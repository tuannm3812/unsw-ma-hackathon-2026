## Answer

**Yes, the negative day-space R² comes from the back-transform, but not in the way
the documents assumed.** The earlier explanation said the model undershoots long
waits. The evidence shows the opposite.

1. The same fit is positive in log space (R² 0.330 and
   0.433). Ridge on raw days is positive in day space
   (0.165 and 0.296).
2. Squared error is mostly over-prediction: 74.7%
   (S) and 69.0% (S+M+L+R). The top 1% of
   predictions carry 55.5% and
   51.4% of it.
3. Exponentiating a linear score produces impossible values: predictions reach
   1,394 and 714 days, against an
   observed maximum of 79.5 days in 2025 and 173.4 in training.
4. Capping predictions at the training maximum lifts R² to -0.079
   and -0.099. Smearing, which scales predictions
   up by 1.603 and 1.508, makes R² far worse.

## What this means for the text gain

- **Text helps under every target on MAE.** The size depends on the estimator:
  0.26 days on the benchmark estimator and
  1.13 days on the raw-days target.
- **No alternative is simply better.** The raw-days target fixes R² and RMSE but
  worsens MAE and median error, because it predicts conditional means of a skewed
  outcome. The log1p estimator stays the better typical-loan forecast.
- **The gain is not robust on every loss.** With the cap, text slightly worsens RMSE
  and R²: its 2025 tail improvement in the uncapped model came from taming extreme
  predictions.
- The larger raw-days and smeared gains start from weaker baselines. Do not quote them
  as a better text effect.

## Limits

One year and one model family. Retrospective, completed loans only. The cap is a
diagnostic, not a proposed estimator; the training maximum is a fixed, training-only
value. Smearing assumes a residual distribution that does not vary with the predictors,
which a skewed duration outcome is unlikely to satisfy. sklearn printed matmul
RuntimeWarnings while computing intercepts on this machine, as in earlier reviewed runs;
the exact reproduction above indicates they did not affect the results. This run has no
independent review. None of it measures what editing a description does.
